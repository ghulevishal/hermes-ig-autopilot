BUILD RECEIPT / 13 OCT 2026

Six slides. *Zero model calls.*

My Instagram carousels are generated by a cron job at night and published without a human looking at them. So I took the image model out of the pipeline entirely and render the slides with 418 lines of Pillow instead.

carousel2026-10-13

The job runs *unwatched*

This carousel is built and published by a cron job at night. Nobody looks at the pixels before Instagram does, and the API has no delete endpoint.

  • Same prompt, different pixels each call
  • On-image text garbles past a few words
  • A sampler gives you nothing to assert
$0.0336

per 1K image on Nano Banana 2.1 — and that model has no free tier at all, so the request returns 429 with limit: 0. Six slides is $0.20 a post, about $12 a month, to draw text a font file already knows how to draw.

What a renderer *guarantees*

418 lines of Pillow, no network. A shrink-to-fit loop drops the point size until the measured block fits its box, so "text is never clipped" is an invariant instead of a hope.

  • 6 slides in 0.37s, no network
  • Re-render is byte-identical: same sha256
  • Palette cannot drift between slides

Where this *stops* working

A renderer cannot invent a photograph or a texture. The split is not model-versus-script, it is judgement versus mechanics, and typography is mechanics.

  • Model picks the story, writes the hook
  • Script owns type, palette, geometry
  • Generate the art. Render the words.

Don't sample what you can specify.
